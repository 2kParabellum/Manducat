Rules

  • 6×6 board, all cards start face down.
  • Each player's army: 1×4, 2×3, 3×3, 5×2, 8×2, M×1. Plus 6 neutral G cards.
  • On your turn you make one action:
    1. Flip any face-down card.
    2. Capture an enemy face-up card with your face-up card along a straight line (like a rook) with no cards in between. You can capture equal or weaker cards only. 1 beats 8. M beats everyone and everyone beats M.
    3. Move your face-up card to an adjacent empty cell (up/down/left/right).
    4. Place M from your hand on any empty cell.
    5. Use your character's ability (once per own turns).
  • A flipped M goes to the hand of the player of its color.
  • A flipped G gives +3 points to whoever flipped it and leaves the board.
  • A captured card gives points equal to its strength (M = ).
  • The first player to reach points wins.
  • Final countdown. After moves in a row without flips or captures, a warning appears and each player gets more moves. Flips and captures no longer stop it. Then the game ends: more points wins; on a tie, whoever made the last move loses.
  • Rule of Three Repeats. If your last 6 moves go A, B, A, B, A, B (e.g. chasing a card back and forth), you can't make move A again right now. It is shown in red.
  • A player with no possible action on their turn loses.
  • Series (Bo3, Bo5): game 1 is played with deck slot #1. The winner of a game keeps their character, the loser switches to the next one in their deck.
